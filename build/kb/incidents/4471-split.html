How a report is placed

  1. shift: the desk read the report as a one-day move (desk-notes.json, oneDayMove). The day of the month is kept, but the invite lands one day early. The bodies say “Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May” and “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday”. A month/day flip cannot make this. Look for a time zone conversion.
  2. hint: a day-first tenant on booking-v3, through the form. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field, so people type the month first, and the form then reads the date day first. The parser fix does not touch this.
  3. parser: any other report from a day-first tenant. The shared parser reads every date month first, so 7 April becomes 4 July.

The tenant decides the cause, not the intake tag. Intake guesses hint from an en-US browser, and that guess is wrong in both directions: the #4471 page shows the tags under each cause.

  • The 5 one-day reports were noted, then counted under parser by tenant. A footnote is not a ticket.
  • The 33 hint reports were marked “not this ticket”, but nobody opened a ticket for them.
